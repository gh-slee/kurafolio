# ANL-108: Compare against a benchmark and show basic risk figures

## Goal

After "what is my return" comes "is that good". Once a valid return series exists (ANL-104), let the user pick a benchmark index or fund and see their return beside it for the same period and currency, with drawdown and volatility where there is enough data.

## Context

### Background

- Waits for ANL-102, where the owner decides whether Kurafolio records transactions at all. The approved baseline tracks holdings and purchases only and the owner asked for "no recorded cash flows" (PRODUCT_CONTEXT.md).
- A benchmark line drawn against a value curve that includes deposits is misleading. This ticket therefore depends on ANL-104 and not on the value chart.

### Scope

- In scope: a benchmark choice from a short list, a comparison for eligible periods, maximum drawdown and volatility with minimum-data rules, tests.
- Out of scope: alpha, beta, Sharpe ratio or any forecast; custom blended benchmarks; claims of outperformance.

### Implementation notes

- Design: read [DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md), [PRODUCT.md](../PRODUCT.md) and [ui-design-workflow.md](../docs/ui-design-workflow.md) first. Keep the existing identity: flat bordered panels, the theme variables in `frontend/src/styles.css`, the existing button, field and status-tag primitives, no new fonts, shadows or colours. A new section goes in the "deeper analysis" part of the page skeleton, below the holdings table, and never above the value and chart. Put the date, currency and unit next to each figure, and show an unknown or partial figure as such instead of a reassuring total. Wording is factual and calm and never reads as trading advice.
- Benchmark prices come through the market data adapter (MKT-009B) as total-return series where the provider has them; say on the page whether the series includes dividends.
- Benchmark and portfolio use the same dates and the same display currency.
- Risk figures need a minimum history (propose at least 12 monthly points) and are withheld below it with the reason.
- Wording is descriptive: "your return", "index return", "difference".

### Where to start

- The return calculation from ANL-104.
- The market store from MKT-009B.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Given an eligible one-year period, then the portfolio and benchmark returns are shown for identical dates and currency.
- [ ] Given a price-only benchmark series, then the page says dividends are not included.
- [ ] Given fewer points than the minimum, then drawdown and volatility are withheld with the reason.
- [ ] Given an ineligible period, then no comparison is shown.
- [ ] No text claims the user beat or lagged the market beyond stating the two numbers and their difference.
