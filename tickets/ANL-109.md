# ANL-109: Plan toward a goal with what-if scenarios

## Goal

A user saving toward an amount wants to see how monthly contributions and an assumed growth rate get them there. Offer a simple calculator on the Total page that starts from the current value and the user's own assumptions, clearly separate from anything recorded.

## Context

### Background

- This no longer waits for transaction records: a projection needs only the current value and the user's inputs. Dependencies changed accordingly on 2026-11-13.
- No owner request is on record. It stays P2 until one is.

### Scope

- In scope: one goal (target amount and date), inputs for monthly contribution, annual growth and inflation, a projection chart with a low, middle and high line, saved with preferences, tests.
- Out of scope: advice on what rate to assume, Monte Carlo simulation, retirement income planning, anything that writes a holding or a contribution.

### Implementation notes

- Design: read [DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md), [PRODUCT.md](../PRODUCT.md) and [ui-design-workflow.md](../docs/ui-design-workflow.md) first. Keep the existing identity: flat bordered panels, the theme variables in `frontend/src/styles.css`, the existing button, field and status-tag primitives, no new fonts, shadows or colours. A new section goes in the "deeper analysis" part of the page skeleton, below the holdings table, and never above the value and chart. Put the date, currency and unit next to each figure, and show an unknown or partial figure as such instead of a reassuring total. Wording is factual and calm and never reads as trading advice.
- Every assumption is visible and editable next to the result. The default growth rate is empty; the user must enter one.
- The result is labelled "scenario" on the chart, the table and any export, and uses a different line style from recorded values.
- Nominal and inflation-adjusted amounts are both shown when inflation is entered.
- A scenario never appears in analysis totals, history or exports of recorded data.

### Where to start

- The Total page from ANL-012D.
- The preferences routes from APP-011E.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Given a value of 50,000, 500 a month and 5% a year for 10 years, then the projected amount matches the compound-interest formula to the cent.
- [ ] Given no growth rate entered, then no projection is drawn.
- [ ] Given zero or negative growth, then the result is correct and the chart remains readable.
- [ ] Given a saved goal, then no recorded figure or export changes.
- [ ] The words "scenario" and "assumption" appear with every projected figure; nothing promises a result.
- [ ] en-CA, zh-TW, zh-CN; inputs are labelled and keyboard reachable.
