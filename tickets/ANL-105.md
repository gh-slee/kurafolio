# ANL-105: Track dividends, interest, fees and corporate actions

## Goal

Dividend and interest income is a main reason people hold funds and bonds, and fees quietly reduce results. Neither appears in a holdings file. Once activity records exist (ANL-103), show income received by month and by holding, fees paid, and handle splits and reinvested dividends so quantities stay right.

## Context

### Background

- Waits for ANL-102, where the owner decides whether Kurafolio records transactions at all. The approved baseline tracks holdings and purchases only and the owner asked for "no recorded cash flows" (PRODUCT_CONTEXT.md).
- Splits matter even before this ticket: the go-live value curve marks a holding "needs a newer import" after a split (MKT-009B, 009D). This ticket is where a split becomes a recorded event.

### Scope

- In scope: income and fee records from activity exports, an Income section (by month, by holding, trailing twelve months), split and reinvestment handling, tests.
- Out of scope: forecast or "expected" dividends, yield on cost unless cost is complete, realized gains and tax lots (RPT-102), withholding-tax reclaim advice.

### Implementation notes

- Design: read [DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md), [PRODUCT.md](../PRODUCT.md) and [ui-design-workflow.md](../docs/ui-design-workflow.md) first. Keep the existing identity: flat bordered panels, the theme variables in `frontend/src/styles.css`, the existing button, field and status-tag primitives, no new fonts, shadows or colours. A new section goes in the "deeper analysis" part of the page skeleton, below the holdings table, and never above the value and chart. Put the date, currency and unit next to each figure, and show an unknown or partial figure as such instead of a reassuring total. Wording is factual and calm and never reads as trading advice.
- Income is shown gross and net of withholding where the source gives both, in the original currency and converted at the rate of the payment date.
- Only received income is shown. No future dividend is inferred from a holding.
- A reinvested dividend is income and a purchase; it is not a contribution.
- A split changes quantity and per-unit cost and leaves total cost unchanged. It is applied only when a source record confirms it.
- Realized and unrealized gain stay "not known" unless lots and disposals are complete.

### Where to start

- The activity records from ANL-103.
- `src/kurafolio/source_builtin.py` for the plugin that would read income rows.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Given three dividends in two months, then the monthly chart and the per-holding table agree with the sum of the records.
- [ ] Given a dividend with 15% withheld, then gross, tax withheld and net are shown separately.
- [ ] Given a reinvested dividend, then income rises, quantity rises and net contributions do not change.
- [ ] Given a two-for-one split recorded by the source, then quantity doubles, total cost is unchanged and the value curve has no false jump.
- [ ] Given a holdings-only user, then the Income section explains that an activity import is needed and shows no zeros.
- [ ] en-CA, zh-TW, zh-CN.
