# ANL-103: Show activity: deposits, withdrawals, buys and sells

## Goal

A user wants one dated list of what happened in their accounts, and a clear figure for how much money they have put in. The baseline cannot provide either, because it imports holdings and purchases only. If the owner chooses to record transactions, add an Activity page and a "net contributions" figure built from imported activity records.

## Context

### Background

- Waits for ANL-102, where the owner decides whether Kurafolio records transactions at all. The approved baseline tracks holdings and purchases only and the owner asked for "no recorded cash flows" (PRODUCT_CONTEXT.md).
- The earlier criteria in [ux-p1-001-activity.md](../docs/ux-p1-001-activity.md) were written for the shipped application; this ticket replaces them.

### Scope

- In scope: an activity list with filters, the classification of each record (deposit, withdrawal, buy, sell, internal transfer), net contributions per account and in total, tests.
- Out of scope: rates of return (ANL-104), dividends and fees (ANL-105), editing records by hand, guessing contributions from holdings.

### Implementation notes

- Design: read [DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md), [PRODUCT.md](../PRODUCT.md) and [ui-design-workflow.md](../docs/ui-design-workflow.md) first. Keep the existing identity: flat bordered panels, the theme variables in `frontend/src/styles.css`, the existing button, field and status-tag primitives, no new fonts, shadows or colours. A new section goes in the "deeper analysis" part of the page skeleton, below the holdings table, and never above the value and chart. Put the date, currency and unit next to each figure, and show an unknown or partial figure as such instead of a reassuring total. Wording is factual and calm and never reads as trading advice.
- A transfer between two of the user's own accounts is not a contribution. Both sides are shown as a matched pair; an unmatched side stays "unclassified" and is excluded from net contributions with a visible count.
- Net contributions are shown only for the period that activity records cover, and the page states that period. Before it, the figure is "not known", never zero.
- Buying Bitcoin with money already counted as a deposit must not count twice.
- Amounts keep their original currency and the rate used for conversion.

### Where to start

- The transaction model that follows ANL-102.
- The analysis contract from ANL-012A and the pages under `frontend/src/v2/`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Given a deposit of 1,000, a buy of 600 and a withdrawal of 100, then net contributions are 900 and the buy does not change them.
- [ ] Given a transfer of 500 between two of the user's accounts, then net contributions do not change and the two records are shown as a pair.
- [ ] Given activity records starting in March and holdings from January, then net contributions are shown from March and marked not known before.
- [ ] Given an unmatched transfer, then it is listed as unclassified and excluded, with the count visible.
- [ ] Filters by account, type and date keep decimal precision and show a clear empty state.
- [ ] en-CA, zh-TW, zh-CN; keyboard reachable; 375 px and 1024 px.
