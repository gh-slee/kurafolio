# ETF-010: Integrate supported ETF exposure and complete country analysis

## Goal

A user sees what they own through their ETFs: top companies, and sector, country and region shares with known and unresolved coverage, from fund holdings that refresh by themselves. This is an epic; the work is in its children.

## Context

- Children:
  - ETF-010A: official fund holdings stored in PostgreSQL and refreshed for held funds. After MKT-009B.
  - ETF-010B: the exposure endpoint. After ETF-010A and ANL-012A.
- The Exposures page that displays the result is ANL-012E, which depends on ETF-010B.
- Existing code the children port: `src/kurafolio/lookthrough.py`, `classification.py`, `fund_sources.py`; tests `tests/test_lookthrough.py`, `tests/test_classification.py`.
- Owner decision 2026-11-10: held ETFs get full underlying holdings, refreshed automatically, in P0. Looking up a fund the user does not hold is P1 (ETF-101).
- Background only: the revision 1 planning draft [docs/specs/v2-drafts/WL-P0-010.md](../docs/specs/v2-drafts/WL-P0-010.md) (sha256:a02f295671c35e3b762a30bc87eb923887e93c54d0007195d5c80072c934149a). Where it differs from this ticket, this ticket applies.
- Golden cases G11 and G12, cited in Acceptance criteria: [docs/golden-cases.md](../docs/golden-cases.md) (sha256:5e6a5db1ec4409cf78f7e1d04a52ee3b66e0079898460199e75fe998c4f71b0f).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The G11 and G12 golden cases give exact US, TW, unknown and cash amounts that conserve value.
- [ ] The top-20 table ranks 1 to 20 with direct, through-fund and total share of the declared denominator.
- [ ] All countries including TW, and separate non-country buckets, remain discoverable.
- [ ] Official effective and retrieval dates and unsupported coverage survive the API and the download.

## Plan

- [ ] ETF-010B exposure endpoint.
