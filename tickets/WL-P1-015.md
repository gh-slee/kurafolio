# WL-P1-015: Show how much the held funds overlap

## Goal

Someone holding three broad funds often owns the same large companies three times without seeing it. On the Exposures page, show for each pair of held funds how much of their holdings they share, and which companies make up the overlap, so the user can judge how diversified the portfolio really is.

## Context

### Background

- Split out of UX-P1-004 on 2026-11-13. It tracks FUT-004 from the old TODO list.
- The data is already there after go-live: WL-P0-010A stores each held fund's published holdings.

### Scope

- In scope: the overlap calculation for held funds, one read route, a matrix and a shared-companies list on the Exposures page, tests.
- Out of scope: funds the user does not hold (WL-P1-002 shows one fund against the portfolio), sector or country overlap, any suggestion to sell or consolidate.

### Implementation notes

- Design: read [DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md), [PRODUCT.md](../PRODUCT.md) and [ui-design-workflow.md](../docs/ui-design-workflow.md) first. Keep the existing identity: flat bordered panels, the theme variables in `frontend/src/styles.css`, the existing button, field and status-tag primitives, no new fonts, shadows or colours. A new section goes in the "deeper analysis" part of the page skeleton, below the holdings table, and never above the value and chart. Put the date, currency and unit next to each figure, and show an unknown or partial figure as such instead of a reassuring total. Wording is factual and calm and never reads as trading advice.
- Overlap by weight between funds A and B is the sum over shared companies of the smaller of the two weights. It is symmetric and between 0 and 100%. State the formula in one line on the page.
- Companies are matched by the classified identity the look-through already uses, never by name text.
- When part of a fund is unresolved, compute over the resolved part and show the unresolved weight beside the result. Do not renormalize.
- Also show portfolio-level concentration: the ten largest companies across all funds and direct holdings, each with the funds it comes through. The Exposures page may already list top companies (WL-P0-012E); add the "through which funds" column there instead of a second table.
- Two funds with different holding dates are compared as published; show both dates.

### Where to start

- `src/kurafolio/lookthrough.py`, `classification.py`.
- The exposure service from WL-P0-010B and the Exposures page from WL-P0-012E.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Given fund A with X 50% and Y 50% and fund B with X 30% and Z 70%, then the overlap is 30% and the shared list shows X.
- [ ] Given two funds with no shared company, then the overlap is 0% and the list is empty with a sentence saying so.
- [ ] Given one held fund, then the section explains that overlap needs two funds.
- [ ] Given a fund with 20% unresolved weight, then the result is labelled as computed on the resolved 80%.
- [ ] The matrix is symmetric and has a table alternative readable by a screen reader.
- [ ] No wording advises selling or merging funds.
- [ ] en-CA, zh-TW, zh-CN; 375 px and 1024 px.
