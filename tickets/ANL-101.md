# ANL-101: Explain a change in value: price, quantity and currency effects

## Goal

When the total moves from CAD 100,000 to CAD 104,000 the user wants to know why: markets rose, they bought more, or the Canadian dollar fell. Between two dates on which holdings are known from imports, split the change in value into the effect of prices, of quantities and of exchange rates, per holding and in total, and show it under the value chart.

## Context

### Spec material

- Background only: the revision 1 planning draft [docs/specs/v2-drafts/WL-P1-004.md](../docs/specs/v2-drafts/WL-P1-004.md) (sha256:ceb1df855357f5892bb15c287a7594a7ee91cf58a49aaca32793f42b89b3de8f). Where it differs from this ticket, this ticket applies.

### Background

- This is the honest version of "performance" that the approved baseline can support. It needs no cash-flow records: a quantity change is shown as a quantity effect, not as a contribution or a return.
- This ticket also carried import-conflict drill-downs and ETF overlap. Conflict review is in the import page (APP-011D) and overlap is ETF-102.

### Scope

- In scope: the attribution calculation between two import dates, one read route, a breakdown table under the value chart on Stock/Bond, Crypto and Total, tests.
- Out of scope: rates of return, contributions, benchmarks (all wait for ANL-102), attribution between dates that are estimates, tax figures.

### Implementation notes

- Design: read [DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md), [PRODUCT.md](../PRODUCT.md) and [ui-design-workflow.md](../docs/ui-design-workflow.md) first. Keep the existing identity: flat bordered panels, the theme variables in `frontend/src/styles.css`, the existing button, field and status-tag primitives, no new fonts, shadows or colours. A new section goes in the "deeper analysis" part of the page skeleton, below the holdings table, and never above the value and chart. Put the date, currency and unit next to each figure, and show an unknown or partial figure as such instead of a reassuring total. Wording is factual and calm and never reads as trading advice.
- Both ends of the period must be import dates, where quantities are facts. If the user picks a range that starts or ends on an estimated day (MKT-009D), snap to the nearest import dates inside the range and say which dates were used. Owner rule of 2026-11-11: nothing is derived from estimated days.
- For each holding, in its native currency: price effect = old quantity x (new price - old price); quantity effect = (new quantity - old quantity) x new price. Convert both to the display currency at the new exchange rate, and report the currency effect separately: old native value x (new rate - old rate). With these definitions the three effects add up exactly to the change in display-currency value. State the method in one line under the table, including where the cross terms go (the quantity effect uses the new price, and the price and quantity effects use the new rate).
- The three effects must add up to the change in value to the last displayed digit. Compute in decimals and put any rounding remainder in a visible "rounding" line, never hidden in a category.
- A holding that appears or disappears between the dates is entirely a quantity effect. A holding with a missing price at either end is listed as "not attributed" and excluded from the three sums, with the total clearly partial.
- Wording: "quantity effect", never "contributions", "deposits" or "return".

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Given 10 units at 100 on the first date and 12 units at 110 on the second, in the display currency, then price effect is 100, quantity effect is 220 and the change is 320.
- [ ] Given a USD holding of 100 units at 50 on both dates while USD/CAD moves from 1.30 to 1.40, then the currency effect is CAD 500 and the price and quantity effects are zero.
- [ ] Given any fixture, then the listed effects plus the rounding line equal the change in value exactly.
- [ ] Given a range that starts on an estimated day, then the period snaps to import dates and the page names them.
- [ ] Given a holding with no price on one date, then it is listed as not attributed and the totals are marked partial.
- [ ] Given only one import, then the section says two imports are needed and shows no numbers.
- [ ] The words "return", "contribution" and "deposit" do not appear in the section.
- [ ] en-CA, zh-TW, zh-CN; the table has a text alternative to any chart.
