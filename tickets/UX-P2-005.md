# UX-P2-005: Export cost and disposal records as evidence for tax filing

## Goal

At tax time a user needs, for each sale in a taxable account, what was sold, when, for how much and at what cost. If Kurafolio records transactions and lots (WL-P2-002, option C), offer an export of those records with the cost method stated, as evidence the user or their accountant can check. It is a record export, not a tax calculation.

## Context

### Background

- Waits for WL-P2-002, where the owner decides whether Kurafolio records transactions at all. The approved baseline tracks holdings and purchases only and the owner asked for "no recorded cash flows" (PRODUCT_CONTEXT.md).
- Risk is critical: a figure that looks like a tax number will be used as one. The acceptance criteria are mostly about what the export must refuse to claim.

### Scope

- In scope: a dated export of acquisitions, disposals, income and fees per account with original and converted amounts, the cost method named, gaps listed, tests.
- Out of scope: computing tax owed, registered-plan contribution room, superficial-loss rules, jurisdiction-specific forms, advice.

### Implementation notes

- For Canadian taxable accounts the usual method is average cost per security across the account holder's identical holdings. The export names the method it used and the scope it applied it to; it does not claim to know holdings elsewhere.
- Any acquisition without a known cost makes the cost of that security "not known" from that point. It is never treated as zero and never filled with a market price.
- Estimated costs (the opt-in proxy policy for Bitcoin) are labelled as estimates and excluded from cost totals.
- Every export starts with a statement: what it covers, what it does not, and that it is not tax advice.

### Where to start

- The lot and disposal model that follows WL-P2-002 option C.
- The export service from WL-P0-013A.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Given complete records for one security, then each disposal shows proceeds, cost by the stated method and the difference, and the figures match an independent oracle.
- [ ] Given one acquisition without cost, then later cost figures for that security are "not known" and the export lists the gap.
- [ ] Given proxy-cost Bitcoin purchases, then they are labelled estimates and excluded from totals.
- [ ] The export never contains the words "tax owing", "contribution room" or a claim of completeness.
- [ ] Registered accounts are listed separately with a note that disposals inside them are generally not reported, and no gain is computed for them.
- [ ] Formula-injection escaping and decimal strings as in WL-P0-013A; en-CA, zh-TW, zh-CN.
