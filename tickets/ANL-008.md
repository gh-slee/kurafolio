# ANL-008: Project source-neutral positions and BTC Option A

## Goal

Add canonical authority selection and a coherent calculation snapshot service for securities and acquisition-only BTC, keeping old report read paths.

## Context

- Golden cases G07 and G19 (G07 in Acceptance criteria, G19 in the OD-13 proposal below): [docs/golden-cases.md](../docs/golden-cases.md) (sha256:f7adca063ff68fe0502066608fc6fad6b1f818244679190b3e7cdcf3020e5594).

### Owner decisions recorded 2026-11-10

- Positions are grouped by asset class, not by institution. A Bitcoin ETF is a security. Coins held directly are crypto whether the record came from an exchange plugin or a broker plugin. Total counts each holding once.
- Tracked crypto quantity is purchases minus sales reported by the same exchange file. Funding rows and withdrawals to the user's own wallet change nothing. No opening balance, wallet check or income. Flag when sales were counted so the page can say so.
- A position missing from a newer broker snapshot is treated as sold when that file covers the position's account. An account absent from the whole file stays unchanged, and the import preview asks whether to keep it or mark it closed (owner confirmed 2026-11-10).
- Book cost and unrealized gain are exposed where the source provides cost (the Wealthsimple holdings file has `Book Value` columns). Unrealized gain is live market value minus book cost. A source without cost yields "unavailable", never zero. Crypto cost is the sum of purchase spending.
- Broker cash is a position class of its own and is part of Total as a separate "Cash" line.
- See [PRODUCT_CONTEXT.md](PRODUCT_CONTEXT.md).

### Proposals OD-05 and OD-13 from the planning review

The Plan's design review checks against these two rows of the unpublished planning review. Both are proposals the review left open for confirmation, not owner decisions, and they add no requirement to this ticket. Where they differ from the owner decisions above, the owner decisions apply. The design review lists each proposed point it relies on for the owner to accept, reject or defer; an unconfirmed point is a question for the owner, not a build failure.

- OD-05, Bitcoin details without reopening Option A (acquisitions are assumed held; no opening balance or wallet input). Proposed: unique acquisitions stay in Crypto and Total; Liquid Bitcoin (LBTC) acquisitions keep counting 1:1 as BTC-equivalent, with settlement and network disclosed separately; completed and corrected purchases and known cost coverage are explicit; a sell signal only warns that it contradicts the assumption. The owner decision of 2026-11-10 replaces that last point: tracked quantity is purchases minus sales from the same exchange file, and the page says when sales were counted.
- OD-13, brokerage cash and unsupported instruments. Proposed: explicitly observed native cash is included at par in account assets and Total, and Bull funding flows are not turned into a cash balance; signed short or derivative facts are kept, and a valuation that is not implemented marks the total incomplete; a mismatch between the corporate-action basis of a quote and of a quantity blocks a misleading current value (golden case G19). The owner decision above on broker cash as its own "Cash" line in Total agrees with the first point.
