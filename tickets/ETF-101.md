# ETF-101: Look up any supported ETF, held or not, and see what is inside

## Goal

The owner asked to search any ETF and see its underlying holdings, not only the funds already in the portfolio (flow step 11 in PRODUCT_CONTEXT.md). Go-live covers held funds. Add a search on the Exposures page that finds a supported fund by ticker or name, fetches its published holdings if they are not stored yet, and shows its companies, sectors and countries, clearly separate from the user's own exposure.

## Context

### Spec material

- Background only: the revision 1 planning draft [docs/specs/v2-drafts/WL-P1-002.md](../docs/specs/v2-drafts/WL-P1-002.md) (sha256:66b813f856dd29d7508985b49e0ed5ed580ce16af46e339cb15d998fa7120a28). Where it differs from this ticket, this ticket applies.

### Background

- Typical use: "I am thinking of adding this fund; how much of it is already in what I own?" That comparison is the value, so the result shows the fund next to the user's current exposure to the same companies.
- Fund holdings come only from the issuers already supported by the fund registry. A fund outside it is reported as not supported; nothing is guessed.

### Scope

- In scope: a search route over the fund registry, on-demand fetch of one fund's holdings into the store from ETF-010A, a fund view on the Exposures page, the "already in your portfolio" comparison, tests.
- Out of scope: adding issuers or funds to the registry (a normal contribution after PLG-101), funds of funds beyond what the look-through already resolves, price charts of unheld funds, a watchlist, buy or sell suggestions.

### Implementation notes

- Design: read [DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md), [PRODUCT.md](../PRODUCT.md) and [ui-design-workflow.md](../docs/ui-design-workflow.md) first. Keep the existing identity: flat bordered panels, the theme variables in `frontend/src/styles.css`, the existing button, field and status-tag primitives, no new fonts, shadows or colours. A new section goes in the "deeper analysis" part of the page skeleton, below the holdings table, and never above the value and chart. Put the date, currency and unit next to each figure, and show an unknown or partial figure as such instead of a reassuring total. Wording is factual and calm and never reads as trading advice.
- Search is by listing identity: ticker plus exchange, or name. Two funds with the same ticker on different exchanges are two results; the user picks one. Never resolve by ticker alone.
- A fetch for an unheld fund is on demand, at most once per fund per day for the whole instance, and shares the refresh process and its limits from MKT-009B and ETF-010A. The stored holdings are instance-wide reference data and carry no user identifier.
- The view states the holdings' effective date and fetch date. If the issuer has nothing newer, say "no newer publication"; do not pretend to refresh.
- Weights that cannot be classified stay "unresolved" and visible. Do not renormalize to 100%.
- The comparison multiplies nothing by a hypothetical purchase. It shows two columns per company: weight in this fund, and weight in the user's current portfolio.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Given a supported fund the user does not hold, when they search its ticker, then its top companies, sectors, countries and regions appear with effective and fetch dates.
- [ ] Given a ticker listed on two exchanges, then both listings are offered and nothing is shown until one is chosen.
- [ ] Given a fund outside the registry, then the answer is "not supported yet" with a link to how funds are added, and no holdings.
- [ ] Given the fund was fetched today by any user, then a second search does not fetch again.
- [ ] Given the issuer page fails, then the last stored holdings are shown as stale with their date, or "unavailable" if none exist; never an empty fund.
- [ ] Every country with holdings is listed, Taiwan included, in the table and the chart.
- [ ] The user's own totals and exposure figures do not change after any search.
- [ ] en-CA, zh-TW, zh-CN; keyboard reachable; 375 px and 1024 px.
