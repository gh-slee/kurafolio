# UX-P1-004: Set a target allocation and see drift

## Goal

A user who has decided how their money should be split (for example 60% equities, 30% bonds, 5% crypto, 5% cash) wants to see at a glance how far the portfolio has moved from that plan and by how much money. Today the Total page shows the current split only. Let the user save one target per asset class and show the difference next to the current allocation.

## Context

### Background

- Target and drift is the feature self-directed investors use most after "what do I own": it turns an allocation chart into a decision aid for the next contribution.
- This ticket used to cover ETF overlap as well. Overlap is a different calculation on different data and now has its own ticket, WL-P1-015.
- The original criteria were written for the shipped SQLite application in [ux-p1-004-allocation.md](../docs/ux-p1-004-allocation.md). This ticket replaces them for V2.

### Scope

- In scope: saving targets per asset class, the drift table and bar on the Total page, the "amount to reach target" figure, locale keys, tests.
- Out of scope: targets per account, per region or per single holding; trade lists, order sizes per security, or anything that places or suggests an order; overlap (WL-P1-015); the shipped SQLite application.

### Implementation notes

- Design: read [DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md), [PRODUCT.md](../PRODUCT.md) and [ui-design-workflow.md](../docs/ui-design-workflow.md) first. Keep the existing identity: flat bordered panels, the theme variables in `frontend/src/styles.css`, the existing button, field and status-tag primitives, no new fonts, shadows or colours. A new section goes in the "deeper analysis" part of the page skeleton, below the holdings table, and never above the value and chart. Put the date, currency and unit next to each figure, and show an unknown or partial figure as such instead of a reassuring total. Wording is factual and calm and never reads as trading advice.
- Asset classes are the ones the Total page already shows: stocks and stock funds, bonds and bond funds, crypto, cash. A multi-asset fund counts as what the analysis contract classifies it as; do not look through it here.
- Targets are whole or one-decimal percentages that must sum to exactly 100. Reject anything else at save time with a message that names the current sum.
- Drift is current share minus target share, in percentage points, and the amount is that difference times the complete total. When the complete total is unknown (a missing price or rate), show the shares over the known subtotal, say so in one line, and show a dash for the amounts. Unknown is never zero.
- Targets are a user preference. They never change a position, a fact, a report or an export figure. Store them with the other preferences from WL-P0-011E.
- Nothing on the page reads as a recommendation to buy or sell. The product reports; the user decides.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Given no saved target, then the Total page offers "Set a target" and shows no drift.
- [ ] Given targets of 60, 30, 5 and 5 and a portfolio of 70, 20, 6 and 4 worth CAD 100,000, then drift reads +10, -10, +1 and -1 percentage points and the amounts read CAD +10,000, -10,000, +1,000 and -1,000.
- [ ] Given targets that sum to 99.5, then saving is refused and the message shows 99.5.
- [ ] Given one holding without a price, then shares are computed over the known subtotal with a one-line notice and every amount shows a dash.
- [ ] Given a change of display currency, then the amounts follow and the percentages do not change.
- [ ] Given user A saves targets, then user B never sees them and no analysis or export figure of user A changes.
- [ ] No text on the page tells the user to buy, sell or rebalance.
- [ ] Keyboard reachable, labelled inputs, no colour-only meaning; en-CA, zh-TW, zh-CN; light and dark; 375 px and 1024 px.

### Tests

## Decisions needed

1. Which classes can have a target. Options: (a) the four asset classes on the Total page; (b) also regions from the Exposures page. Recommendation: (a) first; regions depend on fund coverage and can show unresolved weight.
2. Whether to show "amount to reach target". Options: (a) show it as a plain difference; (b) percentages only. Recommendation: (a), labelled as a difference, never as an instruction.
