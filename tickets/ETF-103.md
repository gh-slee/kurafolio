# ETF-103: Show what the held funds cost each year

## Goal

Fund fees are the one part of investment results an investor controls, and they are invisible in a holdings file. For each held fund show its published management expense ratio, and for the portfolio the value-weighted average and what that is in money per year at the current value.

## Context

### Background

- A portfolio of CAD 200,000 at an average 0.20% costs about CAD 400 a year; at 0.60% about CAD 1,200. Seeing the number is often what prompts a user to look at a cheaper equivalent.
- The fund registry already knows each supported fund's issuer and official pages. The expense ratio is published on the same fund pages.

### Scope

- In scope: the published expense ratio per supported fund with its "as of" date in the reference store, the weighted average and yearly amount on the Exposures page, tests.
- Out of scope: trading costs, spreads, account fees, performance after fees, fund comparison or recommendations, funds outside the registry.

### Implementation notes

- Design: read [DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md), [PRODUCT.md](../PRODUCT.md) and [ui-design-workflow.md](../docs/ui-design-workflow.md) first. Keep the existing identity: flat bordered panels, the theme variables in `frontend/src/styles.css`, the existing button, field and status-tag primitives, no new fonts, shadows or colours. A new section goes in the "deeper analysis" part of the page skeleton, below the holdings table, and never above the value and chart. Put the date, currency and unit next to each figure, and show an unknown or partial figure as such instead of a reassuring total. Wording is factual and calm and never reads as trading advice.
- Store the management expense ratio (MER) where the issuer publishes it; if only a management fee is published, store that and label it as such. Do not mix the two under one name.
- The figure comes from the issuer's official page with fetch date and effective date, like fund holdings. A fund without a published figure shows "not published", and the weighted average is then computed over the funds that have one and labelled partial with the covered share of value.
- Yearly amount = current value in the fund x expense ratio, summed. Label it "approximate yearly cost at today's value". It is not a charge the user will see on a statement.
- Direct stocks, bonds, cash and coins have no expense ratio and are excluded from the average; the page says the average covers funds only.
- No wording recommends switching funds.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Given two funds worth 60,000 at 0.20% and 40,000 at 0.50%, then the weighted average is 0.32% and the yearly amount is 320 in the display currency.
- [ ] Given one of them has no published figure, then the average is 0.20% labelled as covering 60% of fund value.
- [ ] Each fund row shows its figure, whether it is an MER or a management fee, and its "as of" date.
- [ ] Given an issuer page that fails to parse, then the last stored figure is shown as stale, never zero.
- [ ] The page states that the average covers funds only.
- [ ] en-CA, zh-TW, zh-CN.
