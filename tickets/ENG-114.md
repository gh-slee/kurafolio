# ENG-114: Publish the shared spec material that tickets cite

## Goal

About 30 open tickets cite material that exists only on the owner's machine: the untracked V2 planning package under `docs/specs/`, the ignored import contract under `work/`, and golden cases such as G07 and G19. An agent in another tool, on another machine or in another person's clone cannot read them, so those tickets are not complete specs. Make every cited item available as a reviewed, tracked document, or fold it into the ticket that needs it.

## Context

- Rule: a ticket may depend only on tracked documents ([spec-development.md](../docs/spec-development.md#the-ticket-is-the-spec)).
- ENG-016 proposes publishing the whole planning package and has no approval; this ticket covers only what tickets cite.
- Claimable today and affected: ANL-008 cites golden case G07; IMP-007E, 007F and 007G cite the r7 contract bundle.
- Publication rules: [publishing.md](../docs/publishing.md), [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md), `scripts/check_publication.py`.

## Requirements

- Inventory every citation of `docs/specs/`, `work/` and golden case IDs in open tickets.
- For each, publish a sanitised copy under tracked `docs/` and link it from the ticket's Context. Do not edit gated sections, so hashes the owner confirms in ENG-113 stay valid.
- Link documents that set expected values at a commit (or record their content hash), and say in the document that changing an expected value requires re-approval of the tickets that cite it.
- Golden cases keep their IDs, inputs and expected values exactly.
- Nothing private is published: no account names, real amounts, personal paths, transcripts or approval evidence.
- Non-goals: rewriting the planning package, changing any requirement.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] AC-ENG-114-01: No open ticket refers to `docs/specs/`, `work/` or a golden case ID without a link to a tracked document.
- [ ] AC-ENG-114-02: Every golden case an open ticket cites is in a tracked document with its inputs and expected values.
- [ ] AC-ENG-114-03: `python3 scripts/check_publication.py --worktree` and `--staged` pass.
- [ ] AC-ENG-114-04 [review]: A reviewer confirms that nothing private was published.
