# ENG-113: Migrate the approved tickets to the spec format and confirm their approvals

## Goal

ENG-112 made the ticket the spec, with gated sections and an owner-approved hash. The tickets approved before it have neither, so nothing detects when their approved text changes, and six of them changed in PR #64 without the owner confirming the new text. Convert every open approved ticket to the spec format without changing what it means, and have the owner confirm the resulting hashes in one batch.

## Context

- Format and rules: [spec-development.md](../docs/spec-development.md#the-ticket-is-the-spec), [README.md](README.md#writing-a-ticket-as-a-spec).
- Changed in PR #64 after approval: IMP-007E (5 criteria added), IMP-007F (6 added), IMP-007G (3 added), OPS-021 (one criterion and a dependency changed), OPS-015 (dependencies replaced) and MKT-101 (approval carried over from MKT-009E).
- `python3 scripts/tickets.py list` shows the open tickets; those with `gate_approval` set are in scope.

## Requirements

- In scope: every open ticket (not done or superseded) that has `gate_approval`.
- Move scope, non-goals and rules from Context into a `## Requirements` section. Keep the wording; do not add, drop or reinterpret a requirement.
- Number the existing acceptance criteria `AC-<ticket>-NN` in their current order. Do not change their text.
- The six tickets changed in PR #64 (IMP-007E, IMP-007F, IMP-007G, OPS-021, OPS-015, MKT-101) were confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-17 and recorded in their Logs; migrate them like the others, without reopening that confirmation.
- Record `spec_approved` for a ticket only after the owner approves it in words, quoting the words in its Log.
- Do the edits in one docs-only pull request while none of the edited tickets holds a live claim; this is a proposed exception to "edit only your own ticket file", valid once the owner approves this ticket.
- Non-goals: rewriting criteria, migrating backlog tickets without approval (they convert when groomed), publishing local specs (ENG-114).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] AC-ENG-113-01: Every open ticket with `gate_approval` has a Requirements section and numbered acceptance IDs.
- [ ] AC-ENG-113-02: For each ticket the owner confirmed, `python3 scripts/tickets.py spec <ID>` reports that it matches the approved spec.
- [ ] AC-ENG-113-03 [review]: A side-by-side review of each migrated ticket shows no requirement or criterion changed meaning.
- [ ] AC-ENG-113-04: Each migrated ticket's Log quotes the owner's confirmation of its hash.
- [ ] AC-ENG-113-05: `python3 scripts/tickets.py check` passes and the ticket tests pass.
